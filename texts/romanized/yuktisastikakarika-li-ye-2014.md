# Yuktiṣaṣṭikākārikā

namas tasmai munīndrāya pratītyotpādavādine | (0)

astināstivyatikrāntā buddhir yeṣāṃ nirāśrayā |
gambhīras tair nirālambaḥ pratyayārtho vibhāvyate || 1

nirvāṇaṃ caîva lokaṃ ca manyante ’tattvadarśinaḥ |
naîva lokaṃ na nirvāṇaṃ manyante tattvadarśinaḥ || 5

nirvāṇaṃ ca bhavaś caîva dvayam etan na vidyate |
parijñānaṃ bhavasyaîva nirvāṇam iti kathyate || 6

avidyāpratyayotpanne samyagjñānāvalokite |
utpādo vā nirodho vā na kaścid upalabhyate ||
dṛṣṭe dharme ca nirvāṇaṃ kṛtakṛtyaṃ tad eva ca | 11ab (10)

tat tat prāpya yad utpannaṃ nôtpannaṃ tat svabhāvataḥ |
yat svabhāvena nôtpannam utpannaṃ nāma tat katham || 19

na hy atrôtpadyate kiṃcin nâpi kiṃcin nirudhyate | (21)

svena cittena vañcyante bhāvātmānaḥ pṛthagjanāḥ |
bhāvābhāvaviparyāsa-doṣakleśavaśaṃgatāḥ || 24

anityaṃ moṣadharmāṇaṃ muktaṃ śūnyam anātmakam |
viviktam iti paśyanti bhāvaṃ bhāvavicakṣaṇāḥ || 25

apratiṣṭham anālambaṃ nirmūlam anavasthitam |
avidyābījasambhūtam ādimadhyāntavarjitam || 26

kadalīgarbhaniḥsāraṃ gandharvanagaropamam |
saṃmohanagaraṃ ghoraṃ jagan māyêva dṛśyate || 27

brahmādyair asya lokasya yat satyaṃ saṃprakāśitam |
tad āryāṇāṃ mṛṣêty uktaṃ kiṃ na śeṣam ataḥ param || 28

sarvam astîti vaktavyam ādau tattvagaveṣiṇām |
paścād avagatārthasya niḥsaṅgasya viviktatā || 30

viviktārtham ajānānāḥ śrutimātrāvalambinaḥ |
ye na kurvanti puṇyāni hatās te puruṣādhamāḥ || 31

gatayaś ca samākhyātāḥ saphalatvaṃ ca karmaṇām |
tatsvabhāvaparijñānam anutpādaś ca deśitaḥ || 32

mamêty aham iti proktaṃ yathā kāryavaśāj jinaiḥ |
tathā kāryavaśāt proktāḥ skandhāyatanadhātavaḥ || 33

mahābhūtādi vijñāne proktaṃ samavarudhyate |
tajjñāne vigamaṃ yāti nanu mithyā vikalpitam || 34

manovispanditaṃ yāvat tāvan mārasya gocaraḥ | (36)

avidyāpratyayaṃ yasmāt sambuddho lokam abravīt |
tasmāl loko vikalpo ’yam iti kiṃ nôpapadyate || 37

avidyāyāṃ niruddhāyāṃ niruddho yasya jāyate |
tat kathaṃ nāma na spaṣṭam ajñānaparikalpitam || 38

hetutaḥ sambhavo yasya sthitir na pratyayair vinā |
vigamaḥ pratyayābhāvāt so ’stîty avagataḥ katham || 39

ye ’py upādāya bhāvānāṃ siddhim icchanti tattvataḥ |
teṣām api kathaṃ na syur doṣās te śāśvatādayaḥ || 44

upādāya tu ye bhāvān icchanty udakacandravat |
nâpi tathyaṃ na câtathyaṃ hriyante te na dṛṣṭibhiḥ || 45

rāgadveṣodbhavas tīvraḥ kaṣṭo dṛṣṭiparigrahaḥ |
vivādās tatsamutthāś ca bhāvābhyupagame sati || 46

sa hetuḥ sarvadṛṣṭīnāṃ kleśotpattir na taṃ vinā |
tasmāt tasmin parijñāte dṛṣṭikleśaparikṣayaḥ || 47

parijñā tasya kenêti pratītyotpādadarśanāt |
pratītya jātaṃ câjātam āha tattvavidāṃ varaḥ || 48

mithyājñānābhibhūtānām asatye satyasaṃjñinām |
parigrahavivādādikramaḥ snehāt pravartate || 49

nirvivādā mahātmānaḥ pakṣas teṣāṃ na vidyate |
*yeṣāṃ na vidyate pakṣaḥ teṣāṃ pakṣaḥ paraḥ kutaḥ* || 50

kaṃcid evâśrayaṃ labdhvā śaṭhāḥ kleśabhujaṅgamāḥ |
daśanti te na daśyante yeṣāṃ cittaṃ nirāśrayam || 51

kathaṃ sāspadacittānāṃ doṣā na syur mahāviṣāḥ |
audāsīnye ’pi daśyante yadā kleśabhujaṅgamaiḥ || 52

pratibimbe yathā bālāḥ sajjante satyasaṃjñinaḥ |
tathā ca badhyate loko mohād viṣayapañjare || 53

pratibimbopamān bhāvān gṛhītvā jñānacakṣuṣā |
na majjanti mahātmānaḥ paṅke viṣayasaṃjñite || 54

bālāḥ sajjanti rūpeṣu vairāgyaṃ yānti madhyamāḥ |
svabhāvajñā vimucyante rūpasyôttamabuddhayaḥ || 55

śubham ity āgataḥ saṅgo vairāgyaṃ tadviparyayāt |
māyāpuruṣavad dṛṣṭvā viviktaṃ yānti nirvṛtim || 56

naîva raktā mahātmāno na viraktā nirāśrayāḥ |
āśraye sati rāgaś ca virāgaś côpajāyate || 58

viviktam iti yeṣān tu calam apy acalaṃ manaḥ |
kleśavyālākulaṃ ghoraṃ tīrṇās te bhavasāgaram || 59
