# Ratnāvalī

## Abhyudayanaiḥśreyasanirdeśo nāma prathamaḥ

sarvadoṣavinirmuktaṃ guṇaiḥ sarvair alaṃkṛtam |
praṇamya sarvajñam ahaṃ sarvasattvaikabāndhavam || 1.1

dharmam ekāntakalyāṇaṃ rājan dharmodayā(1b2)ya te |
vakṣyāmi dharmaḥ siddhiṃ hi yāti saddharmabhājane || 1.2

prāg dharmābhyudayo yatra paścān naiḥśreyasodayaḥ |
saṃprāpyābhyudayaṃ yasmād eti naiḥśre(1b3)yasaṃ kramāt || 1.3

sukham abhyudayas tatra mokṣo naiḥśreyaso mataḥ |
asya sādhanasaṃkṣepaḥ śraddhāprajñe samāsataḥ || 1.4

śrāddhatvād bhajate dharmaṃ prājña(1b4)tvād vetti tattvataḥ |
prajñā pradhānaṃ tv anayoḥ śraddhā pūrvaṅgamāsya tu || 1.5

chandād dveṣād bhayān mohād yo dharmaṃ nātivartate |
sa śrāddha iti vijñeyaḥ śreya(1b5)so bhājanaṃ param || 1.6

kāyavāṅmānasaṃ karma sarvaṃ samyak parīkṣya yaḥ |
parātmahitam ājñāya sadā kuryāt sa paṇḍitaḥ || 1.7

ahiṃsā cauryaviratiḥ paradāra(2a1)vivarjanam |
mithyāpaiśunyapāruṣyābaddhavādeṣu saṃyamaḥ || 1.8

lobhavyāpādanāstikyadṛṣṭeś ca parivartanam |
ete karmapathāḥ śuklā daśa kṛṣṇā viparyayāt || 1.9

ama(2a2)dyapānaṃ svājīvo ’vihiṃsā dānam ādarāt |
pūjyapūjā ca maitrī ca dharma eṣa samāsataḥ || 1.10

śarīratāpanād dharmaḥ kevalān nāsti yat tataḥ |
na para(2a3)drohaviratir na pareṣām anugrahaḥ || 1.11

dānaśīlakṣamāspaṣṭaṃ yaḥ saddharmamahāpatham |
anādṛtya vrajet kāyakleśagodaṇḍakotpathaiḥ || 1.12

sa saṃsārā(2a4)ṭavīṃ ghorām anantajanapādapāṃ |
kleśavyālāvalīḍhāṅgaḥ sudīrghaṃ pratipadyate || 1.13

hiṃsayā jāyate ’lpāyur bahvābādho vihiṃsayā |
cau(2a5)ryeṇa bhogavyasanī saśatruḥ pāradārikaḥ || 1.14

abhyākhyānaṃ mṛṣāvādāt paiśunyān mitrabhedanam |
apriyaśravaṇaṃ raukṣyād abaddhād durbhagaṃ vacaḥ || 1.15

manorathā(2b1)n hanty abhidhyā vyāpādo bhayadaḥ smṛtaḥ |
mithyādṛṣṭeḥ kudṛṣṭitvaṃ madyapānān matibhramaḥ || 1.16

apradānena dāridryaṃ mithyājīvena vañcanā |
stambhena duḥkulīnatva(2b2)m alpau{sa}jaskatvam īrṣyayā || 1.17

krodhād durvarṇatā maurkhyam apraśnena vipaścitām |
phalam e‹ta›n manuṣyatve sarvebhyaḥ prāk ca durgatiḥ || 1.18

eṣāṃ akuśalā(2b3)khyānāṃ vipāko yaḥ prakīrtitaḥ |
kuśalānāñ ca sarveṣāṃ viparītaḥ phalodayaḥ || 1.19

lobho dveṣaś ca mohaś ca tajjaṃ karmeti cāśubham |
alobhā(2b4)dveṣāmohāś ca tajjaṃ karma ca tac chubham || 1.20

aśubhāt sarvaduḥkhāni sarvā durgatayas tathā |
śubhāt{a} sugatayaḥ sarvāḥ sarvaja‹n›masukhāni ca || 1.21

ni(2b5)vṛttir aśubhāt{a} kṛtsnāt {a}pravṛttis tu śubhe sadā |
kāyena manasā vācā dharmo ’yaṃ dvividhaḥ smṛtaḥ || 1.22

narakapretatiryagbhyo dharmād asmād vimucyate |
nṛṣu de(3a1)veṣu cāpnoti sukhaśrīrājyavistarān || 1.23

dhyānāpramāṇārūpyais tu brāhmādyaṃ sukham aśnute |
ity abhyudayadharmo ’yaṃ phalaṃ cāsya samāsataḥ || 1.24

naiḥśreyasaḥ puna‹r› dharmaḥ (3a2)sūkṣmo gambhīrada‹r›śanaḥ |
bālānām aśrutavatām uktaḥ trāsakaro jinaiḥ || 1.25

nāsmy ahaṃ na bhaviṣyāmi na me ’sti na bhaviṣyati |
iti bālasya saṃtrā(3a3)saḥ paṇḍitasya bhayakṣayaḥ || 1.26

ahaṃkāraprasū{ya}teyaṃ mamakāropasa‹ṃ›hitā |

yathābhūtaparijñānān na bhavaty ubhayaṃ tataḥ || 1.28

evaṃvi{mba}dhārthaśravaṇād dharma(3b3)cakṣur avāptavān |

svaparobhayatas ta(3b5)sya traikālye cāpy alabdhitaḥ |
ahaṃkāraḥ kṣayaṃ yāti tataḥ karma ca janma ca || 1.37

evaṃ hetuphalotpādaṃ paśya‹ṃ›s tatkṣayam eva ca |
nāstitām astitāṃ caiva nai(4a1)ti lokasya tattvataḥ || 1.38

sarvaduḥkhakṣayaṃ dharma‹ṃ› śrutvaivam aparīkṣakaḥ |
saṃtrasyaty aparijñānād abhayasthānakātaraḥ || 1.39

na bhaviṣyati nirvāṇe sarvam etan na (4a2)te bhayam |
ucyamāna ihābhāvas tasya te kiṃ bhayaṃkaraḥ || 1.40

mokṣe nātmā na ca skandhā mokṣaś ced īdṛśaḥ priyaḥ |
ātmaskandhāpanayanaṃ kim ihaiva na te pri(4a3)yam || 1.41

na cābhāvo (’)pi nirvāṇaṃ kuta evāsya bhāvatā{ṃ} |
bhāvābhāvaparāmarśakṣayo nirvāṇa{ṃ}m ucyate || 1.42

samāsān nāstitādṛṣṭiḥ phalaṃ nāstīti karmaṇā(4a4)m |
apuṇyāpāyikī caiṣā mithyādṛṣṭir iti smṛtā || 1.43

samāsād astitādṛṣtiḥ phalaṃ cāstīti karmaṇām |
puṇyā sugatiniṣyandā samyagdṛ(4a5)ṣṭir iti smṛtā || 1.44

jñānān nāstyastitāśānteḥ pāpapuṇyavyatikramaḥ |
durgate‹ḥ› sugateś cāsmāt sa mokṣaḥ sadbhir ucyate || 1.45

sahetum udayaṃ paśyan nāstitā(4b1)m ativartate |

prāgjātaḥ sahajātaś ca hetur āhetuko ’‹r›thataḥ |

asmin satī(4b2)daṃ bhavati dīrghe hrasvaṃ yathā sati |
asyotpādād udetīdaṃ dīpotpādād yathā prabhā || 1.48

hrasve ’sati punar dīrghaṃ na bhavaty asvabhāvataḥ |

evaṃ hetuphalotpādaṃ dṛṣṭvā nopaiti nāstitām |

nirodhañ cāprapañcotthaṃ yāthābhūtyād upāgatam |

dūrād ālokitaṃ rūpam āsannair(4b5)dṛśyate sphuṭam |

dūrībhūtair yathābhūto loko ’ya‹ṃ› dṛśyate tathā |
na dṛśyate tadāsannair animitto ma(5a1)rīcivat || 1.53

marīcis toyasadṛśī yathā nāmbho na cā‹r›thataḥ |
skandhā‹s› tathātmasadṛśā nātmāno nāpi te ’rtha{va}taḥ || 1.54

marīciṃ toyam ity etad iti matvā gato ’tra(5a2)san |

nāstiko durgatiṃ yāti sugatiṃ yāti cāstikaḥ |
yathābhūtaparijñānān mokṣam advayaniśritaḥ || 1.57

anicchan nāstitāstitve yathābhūtapa(5a4)rijñayā |
nāstitāṃ labhate mohāt{a} kasmān na labhate ’stitām || 1.58

syād astidūṣaṇād asya nāstitākṣipyate ’rthataḥ |
nāstitādūṣanā(5a5)d evaṃ kasmān nākṣipyate ’stitā || 1.59

na pratijñā na caritaṃ na cittaṃ bodhiniśrayāt |
nāstikatve ’‹r›thato yeṣāṃ kathaṃ te nāstikāḥ smṛṭāḥ || 1.60

sasāṃkhyau(5b1)lūkyanirgranthapudgalaskandhavādinam |
pṛccha lokaṃ yadi vadaty astināstivyatikramaṃ || 1.61

dharmayautakam ity asmān nāstyastitvavyatikramam |
viddhi gambhīra(5b2)m ity uktaṃ buddhānāṃ śāsanāmṛtam || 1.62

vibhavaṃ naiti nāyāti na tiṣṭhaty api ca kṣaṇam |

dva(5b3)yor apy āgatigatī yat sthitiś ca na tattvataḥ |
lokanirvāṇayos tasmād viśeṣaḥ ka ivārthataḥ || 1.64

sthiter abhāvād udayo nirodhaś ca na (5b4)tattvataḥ |
uditaś ca sthita‹ś› ceti niruddhaś ca kuto ’rthataḥ || 1.65

katham aksaṇiko bhāvaḥ pariṇāmaḥ sadā yadi |
nāsti cet pariṇāma‹ḥ› (5b5)syād anyathātvaṃ kuto ’rthataḥ || 1.66

ekadeśe kṣayād vā syāt kṣaṇikaṃ sarvaśo ’pi vā |
vaiṣamyānupalabdheś ca dvidhāpy etad ayuktimat || 1.67

kṣaṇike sarva(6a1)thābhāvāt kutaḥ kācit purāṇatā |

naiko ’nekaprade(6a3)śatvān nāpradeśaś ca kaścana |
vinaikam api nā‹ne›ko nāstitvam api cāstitām || 1.71
